# Weather SE assets

Loopable OGG ambience used by `WeatherSeService` on **Jukebox track 5** (local-only via `playBuiltInLocal`; does not sync room assignment).

Volume follows the Ambient channel (same as tracks 2–4). Ensure Ambient / music is not muted.

All samples below are from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/) / La Sonothèque by Joseph Sardin, released under **CC0** (public-domain equivalent). Trimmed and recompressed for the app (~16 s, mono Vorbis).
